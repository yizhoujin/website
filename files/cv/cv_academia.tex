\section*{Research}
\subsubsection*{RESEARCH SUMMARY}
\begin{itemize}
%\setlength{\itemindent}{-.1in}
    \item[] I specialize in empirical industrial organization and information economics. My research examines how the commercialization of data and AI technologies shapes information asymmetry (between firms and consumers, and across firms) and the implications for competition, contracting, and consumer welfare.
    I focus on applications in the insurance and digital platform sectors. My methods combine economic theory with causal inference, leveraging administrative datasets, structural econometrics, field experiments, and modern computing.
%I am an economist trained in empirical industrial organization and information economics. I study the commercialization of data and AI technologies and how they create and distribute economic value in markets with imperfect information and imperfect competition. I focus on applications in insurance and digital platforms. I specialize in structural modeling, field experiments, and independent collaboration with firms.
    %I study how data and AI technologies generate useful information in imperfect markets, and the mechanisms through which they create and distribute economic value. I focus on applications in insurance and digital platforms, using large datasets from independent collaborations with firms. My field is empirical industrial organization, and I specialize in field experiments and structural modeling.
\end{itemize}
