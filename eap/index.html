A few notes on the process:
  • Seminars run Mondays, 3:10–4:30 PM, September through mid-May.
  • Approximately 10 external speaker slots per year.
  • We will prioritize internal job market candidates in September/October.
  • We avoid scheduling external job market talks in January and early February.
  • Speakers who cancelled in past years will be automatically added to the voting ballot.
  • We will respect clear winners from the vote, but retain some flexibility to balance gender ratios and, given budget constraints, prioritize Canadian or Northeastern US speakers.
